QUANT

Survey

  • Distributed via Google Forms
  • Cities: Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi and others
  • Topics: safety concerns, feature preferences, willingness to use
  • Analysis: descriptive stats, group comparisons
QUAL

Interviews & groups

  • In-depth interviews and group discussions
  • Explore privacy, discreet design, trust
  • Analysis: thematic coding
MIXED

Pilot test

  • Friends, family and research participants
  • Rate ease of use, accuracy, design, battery
  • Open feedback on reliability
  • Extension: virtual prototype tested online with parents, caregivers and women

Ethics
  • Guardian consent for children and mentally challenged participants
  • Location data privacy, storage and access control
  • Risk of surveillance misuse, so keep the person's dignity central


Limitations
  • Limited face-to-face contact with non-digital users
  • Small, mostly female pilot sample
  • Mentally challenged individuals under-represented
  • Funding, geography and supply-chain constraints


Next steps
  • Engage underrepresented groups directly
  • Test earlier to cut delays
  • Widen geographic scope and address affordability
